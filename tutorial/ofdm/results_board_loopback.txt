First board test of the OFDM receiver (overlay p501_ofdm_rx_sc, 2026-10-24): the host frame (make_ofdm_frame.py: S&C symbol, T1, T2, 44 data symbols, 1 ms) through the DAC DMA as a cyclic buffer,
the AD9361's digital loopback, nothing radiated (TX attenuation 89.75 dB). One process per level: the counters are cumulative, the numbers are the differences over 2 s after the third frame.
Build: FFT=0 OFDMRXSC=1, Vivado 2024.1: 31,353 LUT (26.8 %), 27,570 FF (11.8 %), 9 BRAM tiles (6.3 %), 176 DSP (14.1 %); setup WNS +0.417 ns, hold WHS +0.011 ns, one pulse-width endpoint -0.002 ns (the one of the base design, as in every overlay).
