Why is B210 -> P501 so much worse than P501 -> P501 over the air? 2026-10-23, after the user's report of EVM -12.6 dB (SER 0.22) with a strong signal (AGC gain 3.5)

Reference now (same minutes, notebook stopped): P501 FPGA TX -> P501 RX over the air, RX gain 70: atten 20 dB EVM -22.8 dB (SER 0 in 1.75 M symbols), atten 24 dB EVM -19.7 dB (SER 0).
P501 FPGA TX -> B210 RX with qam16_b210_frame_rx.grc: -19.6 dB at 20 dB attenuation (SER 0 in 7 M symbols).

a level-independent, noise-like impairment that exists only for the B210 -> P501 combination at the moment. One session (the first B210 test, TX 80 to 86 dB, RMS 0.35) had -18 to -24 dB. Not explained yet.
